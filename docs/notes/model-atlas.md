# The atlas has been separated into evidence tests

The former all-in-one atlas mixed fidelity, recovery, architecture effects,
stability, and missing-history diagnostics on one long page. Its URL remains
valid, but each question now has a distinct takeaway and hypothesis:

- [Reconstructing the difference](../evidence/reconstruction.md)
- [Recovering concepts](evaluating-identifiability.md)
- [Held-out signed-endpoint steering](../evidence/heldout-steering.md)
- [Cross-dataset OOD steering transfer](../evidence/ood-transfer.md)
- [Synthetic identification](../evidence/synthetic-identification.md)
- [Direction prediction and transfer](representation-similarity.md)
- [Constraint and training dynamics](../evidence/training-dynamics.md)

Cross-metric exploratory views remain secondary diagnostics rather than a
headline story. This prevents raw-(z) and difference-(\Delta z)
reconstruction from being read as the same estimand.
