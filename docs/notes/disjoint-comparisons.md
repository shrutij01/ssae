# A win count over models that never competed

<p class="lede">Until recently the reconstruction experiment in this repository
concluded: <em>"Across 29 separately scaled dataset, LLM, and reconstruction-target
cells, SAE JumpReLU (penalized) has the lowest mean NMSE in 13."</em> Every number
in that sentence is correct, and the sentence is still misleading. SAE models
were eligible for exactly 13 of the 29 cells.</p>

## The check that should have been run first

The cells partition by reconstruction target. Group them and ask which model
families appear in each:

```python
import csv, collections
rows = list(csv.DictReader(open("tables/reconstruction_summary.csv")))
seen = collections.defaultdict(set)
for r in rows:
    seen[r["reconstruction_target"]].add(r["model_family"])
```

```text
paired difference (delta-z)      families present: ['SSAE']
raw activation (z)               families present: ['SAE']
synthetic observed difference    families present: ['SSAE']
```

The families are disjoint. No SAE in the catalog is ever fit to a paired
difference, and no SSAE to a raw activation — by construction, because the target
is what defines the family. SAE JumpReLU won 13 cells, and there were exactly 13
cells it could enter. Its win rate within its own universe is 13 of 13, and it
had no opponent from the other one in any of them.

Within the difference target the corresponding count is also 13 of 13, for SSAE
variants. The scoreboard reported the first and not the second, because it
counted across the partition rather than within it.

## Why the error is attractive

Three properties made it survive review, and they generalise.

**It was conservative.** The framing of the whole results section was
anti-promotional — the site was written to avoid overclaiming, and a headline
that favoured the baseline felt like evidence of discipline. Caution about your
own method is not the same as accuracy, and it fails in the same direction that
enthusiasm does: both substitute a disposition for a check.

**The disclaimer was present and did no work.** The very same sentence ended with
"raw-activation and difference targets are not compared on one axis." That clause
is true, it is the reason the count is meaningless, and it appeared *after* the
count in reading order. A reader who stops at the number has already formed the
wrong belief; a reader who continues has to reconstruct the implication
themselves. A caveat that contradicts the statistic it accompanies should
replace the statistic, not follow it.

**The aggregation was one line.** The conclusion was generated by
`Counter(row["method"] for row in lowest_nmse).most_common(1)`, which is exactly
the operation you would write if you had not yet noticed the partition. Automatic
conclusion generation is good practice — it stops prose from drifting away from
the artifacts — and it also propagates a framing error identically to every
consumer, which is what happened here.

## The general shape

The failure is a form of Simpson's paradox without the reversal: a statistic
aggregated across a stratification that determines eligibility. It arises
whenever the grouping variable and the treatment variable are confounded by
design. Some other places it appears in this literature:

- **Benchmarks with partial coverage.** A model evaluated on the subset of tasks
  it supports, ranked against models evaluated on theirs.
- **Sparsity comparisons across statistics.** $L_1$ and thresholded-$L_0$ targets
  have different units, so a table of "target 0.005" rows compares runs at
  entirely different operating points.
- **Layer-pooled interpretability results.** Features from a 512-dimensional
  layer-5 residual stream and a 2304-dimensional layer-25 one, averaged.

The structural test is cheap: for each level of the grouping variable, list the
distinct values of the comparison variable. If any level has only one, the
aggregate is describing eligibility rather than performance.

## What replaced it

```text
SSAE variants attain the lowest mean NMSE in all 13 paired difference (delta-z)
cells, most often SSAE JumpReLU (penalized) (10); SAE JumpReLU (penalized)
attains the lowest mean NMSE in all 13 raw activation (z) cells; SSAE Linear
(penalized) attains the lowest mean NMSE in all 3 synthetic observed difference
cells. Reconstruction targets are populated by disjoint model families, so they
are compared only within a target and never across one.
```

Longer, and it says what happened. The generator now groups by target before
counting, and the impossibility of a cross-target comparison is stated as a
property of the design rather than as a hedge.

There is a broader lesson about which errors an anti-promotional voice protects
you from. It catches inflation. It does not catch a mis-specified comparison, and
because its output *sounds* rigorous, it makes one harder to notice — in this
case for as long as the page was public.

## What the experiment is actually for

Reconstruction error, by the argument on
[the theory page](../theory/index.md), is invariant to the mixing matrix
$\mathbf{L}$: a model that blends every concept reconstructs exactly as well as
one that separates them. Fidelity was never going to distinguish the methods.
Experiment 01 exists so that the recovery results in
[experiment 02](../results/identification.md) cannot be explained away by one
family simply fitting its data better — a control, reported as one.
